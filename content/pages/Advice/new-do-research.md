When a student says "I want to do research with you", I need to ask why. What are they intending to get out of the experience? Will this be a good thing for both of us?

1. I am passionate about a topic and I've exhausted the convenional ways to learn about it, and need a **guide/mentor** to lead me beyond where I can get myself.
2. I want something hard/big to work on, and I am open to trying new things to help me figure out where my passions are.
3. I want to explore a topic that I cannot explore in the usual academic settings, and I need some guidance.
4. I want the experience of being part of a project team where I am held accountable. If the team and accountable aren't part of it, then you could do a hobby project.

Practical Reasons (that are totally valid):

1. I need to work with a Professor so they get to know me well enough to write a recommendation letter.
2. I need a pathway to stay in the education system longer (either because I don't want to face the real world, or it's the easiest way to get a way to spend at least a few years in the U.S.).
3. At a big public unviersity, most of the experiences I have a large classes, and I prefer to learn in a smaller setting.
